La-Deidra Blake
Vice President | Operations Manager
Elite Physician Wealth Planning | Fiscal Vision Financial
La-Deidra Blake serves as Vice President and Operations Manager for Elite Physician Wealth Planning and Fiscal Vision Financial, where she is responsible for strengthening the firm’s internal operations, compliance framework, and business processes.
With a professional background in auditing and compliance, La-Deidra brings a disciplined, risk-focused perspective to the organization. Her role centers on helping ensure that the firm operates in accordance with applicable federal and state requirements while maintaining strong internal controls and consistent business practices.
La-Deidra works closely with firm leadership to monitor operational standards, identify areas of risk, and develop procedures designed to support regulatory compliance and organizational accountability.
Her responsibilities include:
	•	Monitoring compliance with applicable federal and state requirements
	•	Supporting regulatory and internal compliance reviews
	•	Developing internal policies and procedures
	•	Creating and improving internal controls
	•	Establishing operational standards and best practices
	•	Reviewing business processes for consistency and efficiency
	•	Identifying operational and compliance risks
	•	Coordinating documentation and recordkeeping procedures
	•	Supporting audit preparation and compliance documentation
	•	Strengthening quality-control procedures
	•	Developing systems for tracking operational responsibilities
	•	Helping ensure that employees understand and follow established procedures
Building Strong Internal Systems
La-Deidra’s role extends beyond regulatory compliance.
She is responsible for helping create the internal systems, controls, workflows, and processes that allow the firm to operate in a consistent, organized, and accountable manner.
As Elite Physician Wealth Planning and Fiscal Vision Financial continue to expand their services, strong operational systems become increasingly important.
The firm may interact with financial institutions, insurance carriers, clients, professional partners, regulatory agencies, and third-party service providers. La-Deidra helps establish the internal structure necessary to manage those relationships effectively.
Her work includes evaluating how information moves through the organization, how responsibilities are assigned, how documentation is maintained, and how important operational activities are monitored.
The objective is to create systems that are not dependent on one individual but instead are supported by clear processes, accountability, and internal controls.

La-Deidra works to develop controls around areas such as:
	•	Client information
	•	Applications and documentation
	•	Workflow approvals
	•	Recordkeeping
	•	Financial and operational procedures
	•	Access to sensitive information
	•	Compliance monitoring
	•	Employee responsibilities
	•	Quality assurance
	•	Management reporting

Her goal is not simply to create more procedures. It is to establish practical systems that help employees work more efficiently while reducing operational and compliance risk.
