Executive Profile
Roderick Johnson brings more than four decades of experience in commercial lending, small-business finance, SBA lending, business development, and financial services to Elite Physician Wealth Planning. Throughout his career, he has worked with entrepreneurs, established businesses, professional firms, nonprofit organizations, government contractors, and other organizations seeking capital and financial guidance to support growth and long-term stability.
As Managing Director of Commercial Lending & Business Risk Solutions, Roderick expands the capabilities of Elite Physician Wealth Planning beyond traditional personal financial planning. His role focuses on an issue that is particularly important to physicians and medical professionals: the financial health of the physician's practice is often directly connected to the financial health of the physician and the physician's family.
Roderick works with physicians, dentists, medical practices, healthcare entrepreneurs, government contractors, and other professional business owners to evaluate the capital and risk-management needs of their organizations. This may include helping a physician obtain financing to acquire or expand a practice, purchase commercial real estate, finance medical equipment, establish working capital, refinance existing obligations, or evaluate SBA and conventional commercial financing alternatives.
Commercial Lending & Practice Financing
Through the Commercial Lending and SBA Financing practice, Roderick helps clients identify financing strategies aligned with their business objectives. His approach begins with understanding the business rather than simply identifying a loan product. He evaluates the financing objective, expected use of capital, financial position, repayment capacity, and the type of lending relationship that may best support the transaction.

A Specialized Focus on Physicians and Medical Practices
Within Elite Physician Wealth Planning, Roderick's role is particularly valuable because physicians frequently operate in two financial worlds simultaneously. They are individuals accumulating personal wealth, but many are also practice owners, partners or shareholders, employers, commercial borrowers, building owners, equipment purchasers, guarantors of business debt, purchasers of substantial business insurance, and future sellers or successors of valuable medical practices.
Elite Physician Wealth Planning is designed to consider these areas together. Instead of addressing the physician's investment portfolio in isolation, the planning process can evaluate how decisions involving the medical practice affect the physician's personal financial future.
Example: A physician considering the purchase of an office building may need to evaluate commercial financing, cash reserves, property and liability insurance, retirement-plan contributions, tax consequences, and the effect of the transaction on personal wealth - all within one coordinated planning framework.
An Integrated Advisory Approach
