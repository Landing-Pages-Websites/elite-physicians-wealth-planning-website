Aliaya Epps serves as Customer Service Manager | Client Experience & Operations for Elite Physician Wealth Planning, where she plays a central role in developing the systems, service standards, and operational processes that support a highly responsive and professional client experience.
Aliaya holds a Master's Degree in Organizational Leadership, providing her with a strong foundation in leadership, organizational development, communication, process improvement, and team effectiveness. She applies that background to the client experience, helping ensure that every interaction with Elite Physician Wealth Planning reflects the professionalism, organization, and personal attention expected by physicians, medical professionals, business owners, and their families.
Her responsibilities extend well beyond traditional customer service. Aliaya helps design, implement, and continuously improve the operational systems that guide clients through each stage of their relationship with the firm.

•  Client experience standards and quality control
•  Internal workflow and process development
•  Appointment and service coordination
•  Client communication and follow-up
•  Document and information tracking
•  Coordination between clients and advisory team members

•  Process improvement and service consistency

Aliaya works closely with the firm's advisors and support professionals to help ensure that important client needs are identified, communicated, tracked, and addressed in a timely manner.
Within Elite Physician Wealth Planning, her role is especially important because physicians and medical professionals often have demanding schedules, complex financial situations, business interests, and relationships with multiple professional advisors. Aliaya helps create structure around that complexity.
Her focus is to help make the client experience as organized and efficient as possible so clients understand where they are in the planning process, what information may be needed, what actions are being taken, and what comes next.
She also plays an important role in strengthening communication between clients and the various professionals involved in their financial relationships. By developing consistent workflows and service procedures, Aliaya helps the firm deliver personalized attention while maintaining the operational discipline necessary to serve clients with increasingly complex financial needs.
