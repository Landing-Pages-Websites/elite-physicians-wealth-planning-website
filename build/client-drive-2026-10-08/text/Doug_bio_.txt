Doug Vincent* has spent the last 31 years in the Financial Services industry concentrating on and working with Pre-Retirees, Corporate Executives, and Small Business Owners in the DC Metro area, with a specific focus on Prince Georges County, MD. 
Doug’s current role as Agency Owner & General Agent, is to cast the vision and lead the daily mission for Greater Washington Financial Services team of comprehensive financial professionals. This Team provides holistic financial and strategic solutions. 
Our Financial Professionals and Support Staff will be on daily mission to build healthy relationships with the community through a systematic process.  Our approach through individual and group meetings will have a specific emphasis on engaging clients to think about what is important to them.  After the discovery, data gathering, and analysis of the client’s profile, prioritized solutions that fit the client’s unique situation will be provided. 
The Ultimate Goal for the Greater Washington Financial Services Team is to be the “Trusted Advisor” for every client.  After implementation, periodic reviews to measure success will solidify the potential for an ongoing relationship.
Doug graduated from Drexel University, in 1992 with two Bachelors of Science degrees, Finance and Marketing. Residing in Prince Georges County for over 40 years, Doug currently lives in the “Oak Creek Community” of Upper Marlboro, Maryland with his wife Angela and daughter Amanda.
