Our Process

The Elite Physician Wealth Blueprint™

The Elite Physician Wealth Blueprint™ is a structured advisory process that helps physicians and medical professionals evaluate their financial life, identify opportunities, coordinate advisors, and maintain a plan that evolves over time.

  1. 01

    Discover

  2. 02

    Assess

  3. 03

    Strategize

  4. 04

    Implement

  5. 05

    Optimize

  6. 06

    Review

01

Discover

We begin by learning your goals, family priorities, income structure, benefits, tax situation, retirement accounts, investments, insurance, estate documents, debt, and practice ownership details if applicable.

02

Assess

We evaluate tax efficiency, retirement readiness, investment alignment, risk management, asset protection, estate planning, practice planning, and cash-flow priorities.

03

Strategize

We develop a personalized plan that connects tax planning, retirement planning, wealth management, practice decisions, and legacy objectives.

04

Implement

We help organize the action steps and coordinate with your CPA, attorney, TPA, payroll provider, insurance professionals, and other advisors.

05

Optimize

We monitor changes in income, family priorities, practice status, tax rules, markets, and retirement projections.

06

Review

Each year, we conduct an Annual Elite Wealth Review™ to keep the plan aligned, updated, and actionable.

The Goal

The goal is not to produce a binder that sits on a shelf. The goal is to create a planning system that helps you make better financial decisions over time.
