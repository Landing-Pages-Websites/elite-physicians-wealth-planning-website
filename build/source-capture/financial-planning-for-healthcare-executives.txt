Healthcare Executives 

## Wealth planning for healthcare executives with complex compensation and benefits.

Healthcare executives often manage a compensation package rather than a paycheck: base salary, annual incentives, equity or phantom equity, deferred compensation, supplemental retirement plans, and negotiated benefits. Each element carries its own timing and tax considerations. 

Planning Priorities 

## What we review
 
 Equity and incentive compensation planning 
 Deferred compensation elections and distribution timing 
 Supplemental and qualified retirement-plan coordination 
 Concentration risk and diversification strategy 
 Multi-year tax planning with your CPA 
 Estate, charitable, and legacy coordination 

Decision Timing 

## Many executive decisions have deadlines

Deferral elections, equity exercises, and benefit enrollments are often time-bound and difficult to reverse. Reviewing them on a planning calendar — before the window opens — is usually more valuable than reacting afterward. 

Coordination 

## One strategy across compensation and personal wealth

We coordinate compensation planning with your investment strategy, retirement income projections, protection needs, and estate plan so each decision is evaluated in context.
