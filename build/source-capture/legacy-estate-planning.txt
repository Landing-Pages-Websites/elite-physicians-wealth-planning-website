Legacy Planning 

## Legacy planning helps connect your wealth to the people and causes that matter most.

A strong financial plan should address what happens during your life, during retirement, and after your lifetime. Legacy planning helps coordinate estate documents, beneficiary designations, asset titling, insurance, charitable giving, family wealth transfer, and liquidity needs. 

Attorney Coordination 

## We do not draft legal documents

We help identify planning questions, organize financial information, and coordinate with qualified estate planning attorneys so the legal documents align with the broader financial strategy. 

What We Review 

## Where financial and estate plans connect

We review beneficiary designations, account titling, insurance coverage, trust funding considerations, charitable planning goals, tax exposure, family communication priorities, and the relationship between retirement income and legacy goals. 

Physician-Specific Concerns 

## Added considerations for medical households

Physicians may have added concerns around asset protection, practice interests, professional risk, blended-family issues, charitable intent, and the transfer of a practice or real estate.
