Founder

Meet Michael A. Epps, ChFC®, RICP®, 2026 5-Star Wealth Manager

Founder & Chief Wealth Strategist, Fiscal Vision Financial

Michael A. Epps founded Elite Physician Wealth Planning™ to give physicians and medical professionals a more coordinated way to approach taxes, retirement, investments, practice decisions, and long-term wealth planning.

Professional Philosophy

A shared strategy across your professional team

Michael's work is built around a simple belief: medical professionals should not have to manage complex financial decisions in fragments. The advisor, CPA, attorney, TPA, insurance professional, and other specialists should be working from a shared strategy.

Planning Focus

Tax-aware retirement and wealth strategy

His planning focus includes tax-aware retirement strategies, retirement income design, wealth management, business-owner planning, and coordination with a client's existing professional team.

Why Physicians

Why the focus is on medical professionals

Physicians often spend their careers caring for others while postponing their own planning. Michael's objective is to help them develop a disciplined, structured, and coordinated approach to building and preserving wealth.

Credentials, in plain language

Credentials, in plain language

ChFC® — Chartered Financial Consultant

Advanced financial-planning credential covering comprehensive planning across taxes, investments, insurance, retirement, and estate planning.

RICP® — Retirement Income Certified Professional

Specialized designation focused on retirement income planning — sequencing Social Security, retirement accounts, pensions, and other resources into a durable income strategy.
