Retirement Strategy 

## Retirement planning for physicians is more than choosing a retirement date.

Physicians often accumulate wealth across employer plans, IRAs, brokerage accounts, practice interests, real estate, pensions, and insurance arrangements. The challenge is turning those assets into a retirement income strategy that accounts for taxes, healthcare, inflation, investment risk, family goals, and legacy priorities. 

Elite Retirement Readiness Review 

## A structured look at what retirement will require

The Elite Retirement Readiness Review evaluates income goals, projected expenses, portfolio structure, savings rate, withdrawal strategy, tax exposure, Social Security timing, Medicare and IRMAA considerations, and estate-planning coordination needs. 

Tax-Aware Withdrawals 

## Withdrawal sequencing matters

The order in which assets are used can affect taxes, Medicare premiums, portfolio longevity, and the amount ultimately transferred to family or charity. 

Practice Exit 

## Retirement planning for practice owners

For practice owners, retirement planning may also involve business valuation, practice sale, buy-sell agreements, succession timing, employee benefits, and the transition from earned income to investment and retirement-plan income. 

Life After Medicine 

## Lifestyle and purpose questions

The process also addresses lifestyle and purpose questions: where you want to live, whether you want to continue part-time work, how much flexibility you want, and what kind of legacy you want to leave.
