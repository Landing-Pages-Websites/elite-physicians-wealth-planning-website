CRNAs, NPs & PAs 

## Financial planning for advanced practice professionals building long-term wealth.

Advanced practice providers often see income rise steadily while benefits, retirement-plan choices, contract structures, and tax considerations grow more complex. Early coordination can create long-term flexibility. 

Planning Priorities 

## Where planning usually begins
 
 Cash flow, reserves, and student-loan strategy 
 Retirement-plan selection and contribution levels 
 Investment strategy aligned to time horizon and risk 
 Benefits, disability, and life insurance review 
 Tax planning for W-2, 1099, and locum income 
 Family, education, and home-purchase goals 

Career Growth 

## Planning that scales with your income

As income grows through additional certifications, leadership roles, independent contracting, or ownership opportunities, the plan should be revisited so savings, tax strategy, and protection keep pace. 

Coordination 

## A structured process, not a product pitch

We start with an assessment of your current picture, prioritize the highest-impact decisions, and coordinate with your tax professional where appropriate.
