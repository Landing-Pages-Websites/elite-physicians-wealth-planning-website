What to expect 
A confidential conversation about your goals and current planning concerns. 
A review of the decisions in motion across tax, retirement, investments, practice, and legacy. 
A clear description of how our process works and what a next step would involve. 

What this call is not 
It is not a product presentation or a sales pitch. 
It is not individualized tax, legal, or investment advice. 
It does not create an advisory relationship or obligate you to move forward. 

Recommended preparation 
A general sense of your income sources and savings rate. 
Awareness of which retirement plans and benefits you participate in. 
Any decisions with an upcoming deadline. 
Your top two or three planning questions.
