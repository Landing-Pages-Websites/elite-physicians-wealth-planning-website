## Coordinated planning for the financial complexity physicians face.

Elite Physician Wealth Planning™ is built around five planning pillars. Each pillar can stand alone, but the real value comes from coordinating them into one strategy. 

Five Planning Pillars 

## Tax Strategy

We review opportunities to improve tax efficiency through retirement-plan design, income timing, charitable strategies, tax-aware investments, business-owner planning, and CPA coordination. Explore 

## Retirement Strategy

We help physicians understand how much retirement income they may need, how to build it, how to withdraw it, and how taxes, healthcare costs, Medicare, and legacy goals affect the plan. Explore 

## Wealth Management

We design and monitor investment strategies that support your long-term objectives, risk tolerance, cash-flow needs, and tax situation. Explore 

## Practice & Business Planning

For practice owners and future owners, we evaluate retirement plans, employee benefits, succession issues, compensation structure, business cash flow, and exit planning considerations. Explore 

## Legacy Planning

We coordinate estate planning conversations with qualified legal advisors and help review beneficiary designations, trust funding considerations, family wealth transfer goals, charitable giving, and risk management. Explore
