Surgeons 

## Wealth planning for surgeons with demanding careers and complex financial lives.

Surgeons often combine high earning potential with unpredictable schedules, significant tax exposure, malpractice and liability considerations, and a career arc that may shorten or change over time. Planning should account for both the income and the intensity. 

Planning Priorities 

## Decisions that deserve structured review
 
 Tax planning across salary, call pay, bonus, and 1099 income 
 Retirement-plan design and contribution capacity 
 Own-occupation disability and liability protection review 
 Investment strategy and tax-aware asset location 
 Cash-flow and savings-rate discipline during peak earning years 
 Legacy, estate, and beneficiary coordination 

How We Help 

## Planning that fits an unpredictable schedule

We build a written plan, prioritize the decisions that matter most this year, and coordinate with your CPA, attorney, and benefits professionals so progress continues without requiring your constant attention. 

Risk & Career Longevity 

## Protecting income and optionality

Because surgical careers can be physically demanding, protection planning and flexible retirement funding deserve early and repeated review rather than a one-time decision.
