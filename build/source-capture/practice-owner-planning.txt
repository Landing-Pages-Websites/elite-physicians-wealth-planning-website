Practice & Business Planning 

## Your practice is more than a business. It is a major part of your wealth strategy.

Medical and dental practice owners face planning questions that employed physicians may never encounter. Retirement-plan design, business cash flow, employee benefits, entity structure, buy-sell agreements, succession planning, and practice exit decisions can all affect personal wealth. 

What We Review 

## Owner-level planning areas

We review owner compensation, retirement-plan design, cash balance and defined benefit plan opportunities, profit-sharing structure, employee benefit strategy, business insurance, key-person protection, buy-sell coordination, practice succession, and exit planning. 

Retirement-Plan Design 

## One of the most important owner decisions

The right retirement-plan design can be one of the most important planning decisions for a successful practice owner. It should be reviewed with attention to owner goals, employee demographics, cash flow, tax objectives, and administrative obligations. 

Coordination Team 

## Planning rarely happens in isolation

Practice-owner planning often requires coordination among the CPA, attorney, TPA, payroll provider, benefits consultant, insurance professionals, and investment advisor. 

Exit & Succession 

## Plan before the decision is urgent

Whether the plan is internal succession, associate buy-in, sale to another practice, or gradual transition, the financial plan should be built before the exit decision is urgent.
