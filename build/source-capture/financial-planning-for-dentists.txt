Dentists & Dental Specialists 

## Wealth strategy for dentists and dental specialists.

Dentists and dental specialists often manage practice ownership, equipment and practice debt, staffing costs, uneven cash flow, retirement-plan decisions, and an eventual practice transition. Personal and business planning are rarely separate. 

For Associates 

## Building the foundation before ownership
 
 Cash flow, debt strategy, and savings rate 
 Employment and compensation-structure questions 
 Retirement-plan participation and investment strategy 
 Disability and liability protection review 
 Preparing financially for a future buy-in or startup 

For Owners 

## Connecting the practice to personal wealth
 
 Owner compensation and business cash flow 
 Retirement-plan design and staff benefits 
 Equipment, expansion, and debt decisions 
 Buy-sell, key-person, and continuity planning 
 Practice valuation, succession, and exit planning 

Coordination 

## One plan across the CPA, attorney, and TPA

We coordinate with your existing professionals so tax, retirement-plan, entity, and transition decisions are reviewed together rather than in isolation.
