Physicians & Specialists 

## Financial strategy for physicians and specialists with complex income and limited time.

Physicians and specialists often earn significant income while carrying limited time to coordinate the decisions that income creates. Employer plans, bonus structures, call pay, 1099 work, insurance elections, and investment accounts frequently accumulate without a single strategy connecting them. 

Planning Priorities 

## Where physician planning usually needs coordination
 
 Multi-year tax planning and retirement-plan contributions 
 Employer benefit and compensation elections 
 Retirement income targets and savings rate 
 Investment allocation and tax location 
 Disability, life, and liability protection review 
 Estate documents and beneficiary alignment 

What We Help Organize 

## One coordinated view of the household

We help organize accounts, benefits, insurance coverage, retirement projections, tax considerations, and family goals into one plan, then coordinate with your CPA and attorney so decisions in one area do not undermine another. 

Why It Matters 

## Uncoordinated decisions cost time and flexibility

When tax, retirement, investment, and protection decisions are made separately, physicians can end up with overlapping coverage, inefficient savings, and avoidable tax exposure. Coordination is the value — not any single product.
