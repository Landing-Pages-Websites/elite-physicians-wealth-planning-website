## Educational use

Website content is provided for educational and informational purposes only. It is general in nature, may not reflect current tax law or market conditions, and should not be treated as individualized tax, legal, investment, insurance, or financial advice. 

## No advisory relationship

Visiting this website, downloading a resource, submitting a form, or attending a webinar does not create an advisory, brokerage, or fiduciary relationship. Any relationship is established only through written agreements and required disclosures. 

## Tax and legal coordination

Elite Physician Wealth Planning™ does not prepare tax returns or draft legal documents. Tax planning discussions are intended for coordination with your qualified tax professional, and estate planning discussions are intended for coordination with a qualified estate planning attorney. 

## No guarantees

No content on this website should be interpreted as a guarantee of tax savings, investment results, retirement outcomes, or financial performance. All strategies involve risk, including the possible loss of principal, and may not be appropriate for every individual. 

## Email communications

If you provide your email address, you may receive educational communications from Elite Physician Wealth Planning™ and Fiscal Vision Financial. You may unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email. Email is not a secure channel; please do not send sensitive personal, medical, tax, legal, or account information by email or through website forms.

If you provide a phone number, Fiscal Vision Financial may contact you about your inquiry. Consent is not a condition of purchasing services. Messaging and data rates may apply if text messaging is enabled. You may request that we stop contacting you at any time.
